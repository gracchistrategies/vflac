Connecting Veterans to Jesus

We introduce veterans to Jesus Christ, and to the miracle of life and salvation. The first help we offer is a relationship with Him.

02

Pro-Life Counseling

We provide pro-life counseling for Pregnant Veterans and their Husbands. The conversation is about carrying the child, and about the support a mother and a father need to choose life.

03

Disability Claims for Combat Veterans

We help Combat MOS Veterans with the disability claims process. The injuries of those jobs, such as the knees of an infantryman, should not have to be argued from scratch.

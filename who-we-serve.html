Vets for Life and Christ serves Pregnant Veterans and their Husbands, Combat MOS Veterans, and Lawmakers and Government Officials who want to understand them.

01

Pregnant Veterans and their Husbands

We serve Pregnant Veterans and their Husbands, whether she gets her care at the VA or somewhere else. Where she goes for the appointment does not change who they are to us.

02

Combat MOS Veterans

We serve Combat MOS Veterans, as each branch already defines that job.

03

Lawmakers and Government Officials

We serve Lawmakers and Government Officials who want to be educated on veterans’ issues and who are excited about the miracle of life. They come to us to understand the people we serve, and the lives at stake.

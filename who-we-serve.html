Vets for Life and Christ serves three groups: pregnant veterans, combat veterans, and the officials who want to understand them.

01

Pregnant veterans

We serve pregnant veterans, whether they get their care at the VA or somewhere else. Where she goes for the appointment does not change who she is to us.

02

Combat MOS veterans

We serve veterans who held a combat MOS, using the definition their own branch already uses. If the service calls the job a combat job, that veteran is who we mean.

03

Lawmakers and government officials

We serve lawmakers and government officials who want to be educated on veterans’ issues and who are excited about the miracle of life. They come to us to understand the people we serve, and the lives at stake.

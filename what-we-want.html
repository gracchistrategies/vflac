Vets for Life and Christ is a veterans service organization centered on Christian faith and pro-life conviction.

Policy Agenda
01

Permanently End Abortion at the VA

Codify in statute the exclusion of elective abortion and abortion counseling from VA medical benefits. Retain only true life-saving care for the mother—ectopic pregnancy and miscarriage management. Taxpayer funds should not be used to end the lives of veterans’ unborn children. Administrative restrictions are not enough; the policy must be locked into law.

02

Mandatory Life-Affirming Consultation

Require that every pregnant veteran or beneficiary presenting at the VA be offered a mandatory consultation with a Christian pro-life counselor who prioritizes the life of the unborn child. The meeting provides a clear conversation about carrying to term, adoption, and available material support.

“My mother never had access to that. No veteran mother should face pregnancy without it.”

03

Dedicated Funding Stream for Pro-Life Services

Establish a statutory set-aside within the VA budget for partnerships with explicitly pro-life pregnancy resource centers, Christian counseling, adoption support, and practical aid for veteran mothers who choose life.

04

Remove Polygraph Testing for Veterans

Prohibit the use of polygraph examinations when assessing veterans for federal employment, security clearances, or VA-related work. Polygraphs lack consistent scientific reliability and impose an unnecessary, retraumatizing burden on those who have already demonstrated integrity in uniform.

05

Introduce Workers Compensation in the Claims System

Introduce workers compensation as a categorical disability claim benefit, paid out for normal, frequent, and expected service-connected disabilities, such as knee pain for infantry.

Why This Matters

The VA was built to care for people who already gave something up. It should not be a place where unborn children are treated as optional, or where the ordinary injuries of service turn into a fight. Vets for Life and Christ is asking Congress to settle both questions in law, clearly enough that veterans and their families can count on the answer.

Support the Agenda

Join us in advancing legislation that permanently ends VA abortion, funds life-affirming alternatives, removes polygraph barriers for veterans, and introduces workers compensation for normal, expected service-connected disabilities.

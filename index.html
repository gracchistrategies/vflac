Vets for Life and Christ is a veterans organization grounded in Christian faith. We believe the people who served should be cared for, and that the lives of their children should be protected.

We stand with veterans when the VA and the claims system fall short, and we ask Congress to settle those duties in law, clearly enough that veterans and their families can count on the answer.

Who We Serve

Veterans of every branch, and the families who carried the cost of that service at home.

What We Want

A permanent end to abortion at the VA, real support for mothers who choose life, and a claims system that pays for the ordinary injuries of service.

How We Help

By taking that agenda to Congress, and by asking partners to stand with veterans while the law is being written.

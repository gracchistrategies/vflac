Vets for Life and Christ

Ending abortion at the VA. Building a permanent funding stream for life-affirming care. Restoring transparency and fairness for those who served.

Vets for Life and Christ is a veterans service organization centered on Christian faith and pro-life conviction. We work to permanently end the VA’s role in abortion, create dedicated funding for explicitly pro-life services, and correct the process failures that leave veterans disadvantaged when they seek the benefits they earned through service.

Policy Agenda
01

Permanently End Abortion at the VA

Codify in statute the exclusion of elective abortion and abortion counseling from VA medical benefits. Retain only true life-saving care for the mother—ectopic pregnancy and miscarriage management. Taxpayer funds should not be used to end the lives of veterans’ unborn children. Administrative restrictions are not enough; the policy must be locked into law.

02

Mandatory Life-Affirming Consultation

Require that every pregnant veteran or beneficiary presenting at the VA be offered a mandatory consultation with a Christian pro-life counselor who prioritizes the life of the unborn child. The meeting provides a clear conversation about carrying to term, adoption, and available material support.

“My mother never had access to that. No veteran mother should face pregnancy without it.”

03

Dedicated Funding Stream for Pro-Life Services

Establish a statutory set-aside within the VA budget for partnerships with explicitly pro-life pregnancy resource centers, Christian counseling, adoption support, and practical aid for veteran mothers who choose life. Ending abortion services without building the alternative leaves a vacuum. This closes it.

04

Remove Polygraph Testing for Veterans

Prohibit the use of polygraph examinations when assessing veterans for federal employment, security clearances, or VA-related work. Polygraphs lack consistent scientific reliability and impose an unnecessary, retraumatizing burden on those who have already demonstrated integrity in uniform.

05

Claims Transparency Mandate

Require that during any Compensation & Pension or disability evaluation the claimant be shown the full claims file and the specific claim under review in real time. The examiner and the veteran must review the same record together. No more closed-door references the veteran cannot see or correct.

Why This Matters

Veterans answered the call. The institution created to care for them should not treat the unborn children of those who served as optional, nor should it evaluate claims in ways that leave the veteran in the dark. Process failures and moral compromises erode trust in the system. Vets for Life and Christ exists to restore both life and fairness through clear statutory fixes rather than temporary administrative policy.
